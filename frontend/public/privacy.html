Privacy Policy

Shift Handover · Last updated 15 November 2026

Shift Handover is a student final-year project at Strathmore University, built as a proof of concept for multilingual shift handovers in airline ground and maintenance operations. It is not operated by, endorsed by or affiliated with Kenya Airways or any airline. Please do not enter real operational, safety or personal data.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, role, and, if a supervisor adds them, staff number, station and rostered shift. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose "Sign in with Google", we receive your name, email address and Google account identifier from Google. We do not receive your Google password or access your Gmail, Drive or contacts.
  • Handovers: the text you submit, and the results produced from it (incident category, confidence, extracted entities, summary), together with the tasks, alerts and reviews linked to it.
  • Voice input: recordings are sent to our server only to be transcribed into text. The audio is processed in memory and is not stored; only the text you then submit is kept.
  • Technical data: your IP address is used briefly to limit repeated sign-in and password-reset attempts. Standard server logs may record requests for troubleshooting.

How we use it

Only to run the service: to sign you in, analyse and store handovers, assign and track follow-up tasks, send alerts, and send password-reset emails. We do not sell your data, show advertising, or use it for anything outside this project.

Who processes it

  • Microsoft Azure hosts the server and database.
  • Google verifies Google sign-in, and Gmail sends password-reset emails.

Handover text is analysed by our own model on our own server; it is not sent to any outside AI service.

Storage and retention

Data is kept for as long as the project runs so that handovers remain traceable. An account that has records is deactivated or anonymised rather than deleted. The project and its data will be shut down once the project has been assessed.

Your choices

You can ask for your account to be deactivated or anonymised, or ask what data we hold about you, by emailing shifthandover.app@gmail.com. You can remove Shift Handover's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account settings under "Third-party apps and services".

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, access is restricted by role, and sign-in sessions can be revoked. No system is perfectly secure, which is another reason not to enter real data.

Contact

Questions about this policy: shifthandover.app@gmail.com.
